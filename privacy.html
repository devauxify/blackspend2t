Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

SENDALL ("we," "us," "our") sends a free email newsletter. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or subscribe, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our site or subscribing, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Email address. When you subscribe, you give us your email address. This is the only information the signup form asks for.
  • Subscription and engagement data. Our email provider records when you subscribed and may record whether you open our emails and click links.
  • Technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type and pages visited.

How we use your information

  • To send you our newsletter and related emails you signed up for, such as a welcome message, deals and opportunities.
  • To confirm your subscription and manage your preferences.
  • To understand which content is useful and improve our emails and website.
  • To keep our site secure and prevent spam or abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your email address or personal information.

Service providers we use

We rely on a few trusted companies to run our site and emails. They process information on our behalf and under their own privacy policies:

  • Mailchimp (Intuit) stores our subscriber list and sends our emails.
  • Cloudflare hosts and delivers our website.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces used on our site, which means your browser may contact Google's servers when you load a page.

We may also share information if required by law, to enforce our rights, or to protect the safety of our users.

Cookies and tracking

Our website itself does not use advertising cookies. Our email provider may use tracking technology, such as small images and link tracking, to measure whether emails are opened and which links are clicked. Our hosting and font providers may set or read limited technical data needed to deliver the site.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe any time. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link at the bottom. You can also email us and we will remove you.
  • Access, correction and deletion. You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us at the address below.
  • Your rights. Depending on where you live (for example, California and certain other U.S. states), you may have additional rights over your personal information, including the right to know, delete and correct it, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We honor these requests where they apply.

Data retention and security

We keep your email address for as long as you remain subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we keep a minimal record so we do not email you again, and we delete other information when it is no longer needed. We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children's privacy

Our newsletter is intended for people 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us their information, contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other sites

Our emails and website may link to other websites, including Black-owned businesses and third-party offers. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of our site or newsletter after a change means you accept the updated policy.
